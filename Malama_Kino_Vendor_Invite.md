# Mālama Kino Vendor Invitation

Aloha [Business Name],

We're hosting Mālama Kino, a community Health Fair for Hawaii Islanders in Waikoloa Village, on Saturday, January 23rd, 2027 from 9am–12pm at Waikoloa Elementary School. We'd love for you to be part of it.

Mālama Kino (Caring For the Body) brings together local health providers, wellness practitioners, and community organizations for a morning centered on whole-person wellness. With around 200 community members expected, it's a great chance to connect directly with Waikoloa Village neighbors and families, share what you offer, and be part of something that gives back to the community.

We have space reserved for a limited number of vendor/exhibitor tables ($35 per table), and we'd be honored to have [Business Name] represented. Setup is easy: we handle the venue logistics, you bring your table(s), materials, and team.

**Mālama Kino Passport.** We plan on having a Health Fair Passport to help guests visit every table. Each attendee will receive a passport card listing all participating organizations along with their phone numbers, so your business stays in guests' hands long after the event. As guests stop by your table, you simply sign their passport in your booth signature space. Guests who collect signatures from every participating organization can turn in their completed passport for entry into our prize raffle, drawn at 11:45am.

**Raffle Prize Donations.** To make the raffle something guests are excited to work toward, we're inviting participating organizations to donate a prize. Ideas include a gift card, a service or class voucher, a wellness product basket, or anything that showcases what you offer. Donations are optional and greatly appreciated, and each donor will be recognized by name at the raffle drawing and on event signage. If you'd like to contribute, just let us know what you have in mind.

Would you be open to a quick call this month to talk through details, including the passport and raffle? Happy to work around your schedule.

Looking forward to hearing from you.
